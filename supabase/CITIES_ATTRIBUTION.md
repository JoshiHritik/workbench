## City data source

`world_cities.csv` (90,886 cities, every country) is derived from the
[Countries States Cities Database](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database)
by dr5hn, licensed under the [Open Database License (ODbL)](https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database/blob/master/LICENSE).

Filtered to `type = "city"` rows only (excludes administrative
subdivisions like districts/counties), with the 10 curated GlobeTrotter
cities excluded to avoid duplicates with their photographed entries.
